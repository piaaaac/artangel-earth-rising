It begins in deliberate dissonance. The musicians enter one by one, starting with the first violin, playing markedly out of tune. Over the opening section they gradually converge, tuning across the same bars until, in a single shared moment, they strike the first consonant harmony. From that point of arrival, the music continues to build steadily in intensity through passages of reflection, sadness and growing force. For Sawhney, the piece seeks to juxtapose the pain and chaos of our isolated lives against the quiet power of community — the beauty and grace that arise when disparate voices find common ground.
