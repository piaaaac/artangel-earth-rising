Blocksright: [{"content":{"text":"<p>ABOUT THE TRACK</p><hr><p>Sharda Patasar’s Audacity of Black Water is a composition inspired by a folk song her grandfather would sing. The key refrain: ‘Hold meh hand gyul meh finger full ah gold ring’ is performed instrumentally. When passing the song down, her father taught it as a playful rasa, or essence, of Raga Peelu. Patasar has added her own interpretation, employing shadows of Trinidadian folk and popular music, with sitar and bass guitar. The former is played using conventional and unconventional techniques. The unconventional creates colours from the string sounds that mimic a morchang or the jaw harp to percussion. Into this mix she adds African conga drums and the Indian Dholak. Patasar’s experimentation with sitar as a musical instrument and sound tool began over a decade ago, and reflects her ongoing preoccupation with sound identities, diaspora and the value of not belonging.</p>"},"id":"99bee2b0-01e7-48ca-a150-1a59e18e79e2","isHidden":false,"type":"text"}]
