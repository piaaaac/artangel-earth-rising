Born and raised in Trinidad and Tobago, he holds Bachelor of Music degrees in Jazz Performance and Ethnomusicology from Oberlin Conservatory of Music, and a Masters of Music in Jazz Studies from New York University. Rodulfo focuses on publicly engaging work addressing decolonisation, the intersection of traditional and contemporary music, and ideas of transcendence. His work employs electro-acoustic music composition and improvisation, applying multimedia approaches to live performance and installation.
